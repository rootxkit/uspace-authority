# WP-16: certificates, the public register and operating status

Branch `feat/WP-16-certificates`. Milestone A-M4. Owns
`internal/certs`, the `certificates` migration, the routes
`/v1/certificates/*`, the `ussp_list` dataset for F1 and the lapse job.
Depends on WP-2 (clients are created from certificates), WP-6 (the
outbox). Consumers: WP-14 (USSP base URLs and status), WP-17 (records
fetch), WP-22.

## Read first

1. `docs/PLAN.md` §4.1 `certificates`, §5 certificate rows, §14 Q-A18.
2. Spec `01` A5 (certify, public register, oversight, notices, lapse
   rules), `02 F1` (the USSP list fields: name, contact, certificate id,
   base URL, services, certification limitations, validity, `terms_url`),
   `02 F7` operating status (`POST /v1/certificates/{id}/status`),

3. LESSONS E-01, E-02, INV-03 (the 6 / 12 month figures are policy
   columns, not literals).

## What to build

- `certificates`: `holder` USSP/CISP, `holder_name`, `address`,
  `contact`, `code` (≤ 8 upper-case alphanumerics, unique, assigned at
  issue and never changed; it is `ussp_id` in `cis/ussp_list/v1`, the
  USSP's `USSP_SYSTEM_ID`, the short USSP code of the authorisation
  number in `03 §6`, and the `<code>` in the client id `ussp-<code>-01`;
  `USSP-DEV` in the lab; M8, M24), `client_id` (FK to `oauth_clients`),
  `base_url`,
  `services[]` (the Annex VI list: NID, GEO, FA, TI, WX, CM, CIS),
  `conditions`, `limitations`, `terms_url`, `issued_at`, `valid_until`,
  `status` issued/operating/ceased/suspended/limited/revoked/lapsed,
  `operations_started_at`, `operations_ceased_at`, `lapse_unused_after`
  and `lapse_ceased_after` (defaults 6 and 12 months from policy),
  every transition audited with the actor and reason.
- Lifecycle: issue (creates the `oauth_clients` row with the scopes the
  holder's services imply, least privilege: a USSP gets
  `registry.validate`, `occurrences.write`, `certificates.status`,
  `rid.service_provider`, the F3548 scopes its services need; a CISP
  gets `cis.*` as publisher-side; the secret or JWKS handed over out of
  band), suspend (client status → suspended at once; a token already
  issued stays valid to its TTL and the console says so; the USSP list
  republished without the holder), limit, revoke, reinstate.
- Operating status (`02 F7`): `POST /v1/certificates/{id}/status
  {state: started|ceased|restarted, at, reference}` with scope
  `certificates.status` from the holder's own client (the `sub` must
  equal the certificate's `client_id`; a test refuses another client's
  token beside the accepted one); recorded, audited; manual entry by an
  admin for the paper fallback.
- Lapse job (`api`, daily, advisory lock): `issued` and never started
  within `lapse_unused_after` → `lapsed`; `ceased` for
  `lapse_ceased_after` → `lapsed`; each a transition with the rule
  named; idempotent; E-01 test with both a lapsing and a non-lapsing
  certificate.
- Public register `GET /v1/certificates/register` (Art. 18(a)):
  holder, services, status, validity, limitations; no contact person,
  no client ids; cacheable, rate-limited.
- `ussp_list` dataset: built from `operating` and `limited` USSP
  certificates to the CISP's `cis/ussp_list/v1` schema (the CISP owns
  it, M7; `ussp_id` = `code`), validated in CI against the pinned copy
  WP-6 carries under `api/clients/cisp-schemas/` + `SOURCE`, and
  published through WP-6's outbox on every relevant transition and on
  `POST /v1/certificates/publish-list`.

## Tests

- Unit: the transition graph (every refused transition beside an
  accepted one); scope derivation per service set; the register
  excludes PII fields (schema grep as in WP-3); `code` refused when
  longer than 8, lower-case, or already used, accepted otherwise
  (E-01); the list validates against the pinned `cis/ussp_list/v1`.
- Integration: issue → client usable (token obtained with WP-2) →
  suspend → token refused at the next request and the list republished
  without the holder; status notices by the right client and refused for
  another; the lapse job on backdated rows.
