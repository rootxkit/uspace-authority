## What to build

- Incidents: `kind` (airprox, nonconformance, lost_link, emergency,
  violation_escalated, other), `occurred_at`, `opened_from`
  (violation / own observation / ansp notice / ussp notice), aircraft
  rows (serial, operator registration public part, track ids,
  identification at the time), `intent_refs[]`, `narrative`, `severity`,
  `status` open/assigned/closed, `assignee`, notes (append-only); every
  change audited. `POST /v1/violations/{id}/review` with `escalated`
  calls `incidents.OpenFromViolation`.
- Evidence pack builder: given an incident and a window, assembles a
  manifest of track excerpts (from `tracks`, `rid_observations` with the
  raw frames, `ussp_flights` if still within 24 h, `manned_tracks`),
  the zone versions in force (`proj_zones` / `geo_zones` by version),
  the policy version, the violation rows, the relevant `events` lines,
  the ground dataset and spacing for every AGL number (D-05), and the
  USSP service record fetched on demand (`GET /v1/records/flights/{id}`
  from the USSP's `base_url` with scope `ussp.records`; absent → the
  manifest says `ussp_record: unavailable (reason)`, never silently
  missing). Tracks are rendered as segments cut by silence > 3 s, by
  recorded gaps (`writer_gaps`) and by samples without a position, each
  hole labelled with its cause or "no recorded cause" (B-13). A window
  above a configured size is refused, not thinned.
- Pack kinds: `oversight` (PII redacted: registration public part and
  serial only) and `legal` (PII resolved from the registry, purpose and
  case reference mandatory). The pack is a deterministic archive
  (sorted entries, fixed timestamps) stored under `EVIDENCE_DIR` or an
  S3-compatible bucket (config), `content_hash` = SHA-256 of the
  archive recorded in `evidence_packs` and in an `events` row; every
  download audited with purpose; `GET .../verify` recomputes the hash.
- Routes per plan §5.

## Tests

- Unit: segmenting with each hole cause (E-01: a continuous track
  yields one segment); redaction rules per pack kind; deterministic
  archive (two builds, equal bytes); hash verification detects a
  modified byte.
- Integration: a violation from the WP-12 scenario escalated → incident
  → pack built with raw frames and zone versions → hash recorded →
  download audited → verify passes; the alert store made unreadable →
  the pack says `violations: unavailable` (B-13); the fake USSP's record
  endpoint down → `ussp_record: unavailable`.
