# WP-26: the no-authorisation detector and U-space gating

Branch `feat/WP-26-no-authorisation-detector`. Milestone A-M5. **Gated**
on an answer to spec `08` Q2 (a designated U-space airspace) and plan
§14 Q-A5 (the DSS scope). Owns the `no_authorisation` violation kind,
the `height_limit_in_uspace = skip_when_authorised` behaviour, the

## What to build

- Intent discovery: per `USPACE` zone in force, query the DSS for
  operational intent references intersecting the volume and the next
  hour (`utm.conformance_monitoring_sa`, Q-A5), fetch details from each
  `uss_base_url` (`GET /uss/v1/operational_intents/{entityid}`), keep
  them in a bounded cache ≤ 24 h, refreshed by a DSS subscription and
  periodic re-query; `dss_unavailable` state shown and the detector
  suspended (not "clear") while the DSS is unreachable (E-02).
- Matching: a track inside a `USPACE` zone (horizontally and
  vertically, through `zones`) at `captured_at` is matched to an
  `Activated` intent whose `Volume4D` contains it (envelope prefilter
  from `f3548`, exact test through `geodesy`) and whose operator
  registration (from the details' Annex IV block, if the USSP exposes
  it, else the authorisation number's operator part) or serial matches
  the track's identification; no match for longer than a policy grace
  (default 10 s) → `no_authorisation` raised with the zone, the
  candidates considered and why each failed; cleared when matched or on
  exit, with hysteresis.
- Height gating: with `height_limit_in_uspace = skip_when_authorised`,
  `height_120m` is not evaluated for a track matched to an intent
  (the USSP's conformance covers it); unmatched tracks inside U-space
  are still judged against 120 m and `no_authorisation`.
- Everything on the status line: zones watched, intents cached, DSS
  state, matches, grace timers.

## Tests

- Unit: matching with the intent and track from the fake DSS/USSP
  doubles; every "why it failed" reason beside a match (E-01).
- Scenario: an aircraft inside a `USPACE` zone with an `Activated`
  intent raises nothing; the intent ended → `no_authorisation` after the
  grace, cleared on exit; the DSS taken down → the detector reports
  suspended and raises nothing, then resumes (E-02).
- E-10: intent cache bound and 24 h purge proven on a backdated entry.
