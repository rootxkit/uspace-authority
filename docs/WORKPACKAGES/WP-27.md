# WP-27: retention, archive and record verification

Branch `feat/WP-27-retention-archive`. Milestone A-M5. Owns the archive
job, the 90-day retention policies that WP-9 deferred, the PII drop
from archived telemetry, the hash-chain verification job and
`/v1/audit/verify`, and the daily USSP records pull. Depends on WP-1,
WP-9 (and WP-16 for USSP base URLs, WP-17 for where records go).

## Read first

1. `docs/PLAN.md` §4, §7 (records), §14 Q-A15, Q-A18.
2. Spec `05 §4` (every row of the retention table; the authority's
   figures are national choices pending the DPO), `06 §2` T7 (periodic
   hash verification, backups to a second account), `06 §5` (storage
   limitation; the remote pilot position dropped from the long-term
   archive after 90 days unless an incident references it), `02 F7`
   (records daily bundles), `08` Q8.
3. LESSONS E-02 (a cleanup that reports success while doing nothing is
   worse than a failure), E-10, INV-03 (periods are policy rows).

## What to build

- Archive job (`api`, daily, advisory lock): for each hypertable with
  a 90-day online window, export chunks older than the window to
  Parquet (or newline-delimited JSON gzip if a Parquet writer would add
  a dependency the owner declines; record the choice) in object storage
  (`ARCHIVE_URL`, S3-compatible or a local directory), verify the
  object's checksum after upload, **then** drop the chunk; the job
  prints rows exported, bytes, and the chunk names (never "done" without
  numbers). `ussp_flights` is never archived (24 h disposal). Operator
  and remote-pilot positions (the `operator_location` column of
  `ussp_flights` excerpts copied into violations, and any such field in
  archived tracks) are nulled in the archive unless an incident
  references the track in the window.
- Retention policies: add the 90-day `drop_chunks` policies for
  `rid_observations`, `tracks`, `manned_tracks` only after the archive
  job has run successfully for that chunk (the policy is applied by the
  job, not by a blanket Timescale policy, so nothing is dropped
  unarchived).
- Hash-chain verification job (monthly and on demand through
  `GET /v1/audit/verify?month=`): runs `audit.Verify`, records the
  result as an `events` row (so the verification is itself in the
  chain), alarms on a break.
- Evidence-pack re-verification (monthly): recompute each pack's hash
  against storage and record the result.
- USSP records daily pull (Q-A18): for every operating USSP, `GET
  /v1/records/daily/{date}` with scope `ussp.records` into the archive
  store; a missing day is an alarm on the console (`02 F7`).
- Periods from `authority_policy` (`online_days`, `archive_years`,
  `violations_years`, `audit_years`) with the `05 §4` defaults.

## Tests

- Integration: backdated chunks archived with the right counts and
  then dropped; a failed upload leaves the chunk in place and reports
  it (E-01 beside the success); the PII null rule applied and, for a
  track an incident references, not applied; the verification job
  passes on a clean chain, detects a tampered row, and writes its own
  `events` row; the daily records pull with the fake USSP and the
  missing-day alarm.
