## What to build

### `cmd/detect` and `internal/detectsvc`

- One worker per owned `cell3` (KV `cells`; `CELLS=all` for the demo):
  a JetStream pull consumer on `trk.v1.<cell3>.>` (durable per worker,
  explicit ack, bounded pending), one `alerting.Monitor` per `cell3`
  built from `DefaultConfig` overridden by the policy follower
  (`PressureUncertaintyM`, `ZonePolicy.MaxHeightAGLM` = 120 from policy,
  severities, `ClearAfterS`, `StaleAfterS`, `LiveMaxAgeS`,
  `MismatchSeverity`, `IdentificationSeverity`) and the zones from the
  WP-5 projection reader plus the WP-6 restriction projection, rebuilt
  on `zones.v1.changed` / `cis.v1.restrictions` within one tick (Z-12)
  and on policy activation (a config change rebuilds the monitor and
  re-observes the last track per aircraft, INV-03).
- Track → `alerting.Track`: `Env` from `internal/ground` for the track's
  position (cached per cell and position bucket, off the hot path, C-13),
  `Flying`, `AltAMSLM`/`AltSource`, velocities, times (`CapturedAtS`,
  `RxAtS`, `SourceTS`), `Backlog`, `Source`, `Station` (receiver or
  USSP), `Transmitter`, `Identification`. Wall time from the process
  clock; `Tick` every second.
- Event mapping (D5): `Raised` of kind `height` → `height_120m`
  (`peak_value` = `height_agl_m`, `terrain_source` = dataset and
  spacing), `zone` → `zone_incursion` (with `vertical_known`,
  `limit_not_judged`, `not_judged`, `within_band`), `identification` →
  `unregistered` (the unidentified / unknown-operator aircraft in a
  PROHIBITED or REQ_AUTHORIZATION zone, G-03), `identification_mismatch`
  → `identification_mismatch`; `conflict` → discarded, counted
  `conflict_events_ignored` (Q-A9). `Cleared` events carry the clear
  reason (`resolved`, `stale`, `source_disabled`, `landed`) and the
  numbers at clearing (C-14). Each raise copies the triggering track
  samples (last 10 s for the aircraft) into `evidence_excerpt`, because
  the DP cache will be gone in 24 h (`03 §1`).
- `violation/v1` published on `alrt.v1.<kind>.<cell5>.<violation_id>`
  with `state` raised/updated/cleared, `policy_version`, `captured_at`
  of the triggering sample, `evidence_refs[]` (track ids, receiver ids,
  zone identifier and version); active violations republished every 1 s
  with current numbers (C-08).
- Source switches: the `sources.Follower` of WP-10 calls
  `Monitor.SwitchSource` on change, clearing as `source_disabled` at once
  (SC-08, B-11).
- Status line every interval with the monitor's counters
  (`rejected_backlog`, `rejected_late`, `rejected_out_of_order`,
  `rejected_source_disabled`, `zone_checks_not_evaluated`,
  `zone_limits_not_judged`, `conflict_events_ignored`, tracked,
  capacity) and, **at error level** while any PROHIBITED or
  REQ_AUTHORIZATION zone needs terrain or geoid that is not configured,
  the list of such zones (Z-09, SC-13, SC-22).
- `height_limit_in_uspace` policy: `evaluate` (default; everywhere)
  or `skip_when_authorised` (only with WP-26); inside a `USPACE` zone
  the detector records `in_uspace: true` on the violation either way.

### `internal/violations` (in `api`)

- Consumer of `alrt.v1`: `raised` → insert `violations` (`status: new`,
  `opened_at`), `updated` → `peak_value` and excerpt append (bounded),
  `cleared` → `closed_at` and `clear_reason`; idempotent on
  `violation_id` (JetStream redelivery). Every transition an `events`
  row. Never any read of `occurrences.*` (the schema has no grant for
  this role; a test proves it).
- Review: `POST /v1/violations/{id}/review` with `reviewed` /
  `dismissed` / `escalated` (+ note); `escalated` opens an incident
  through WP-17's interface (until it merges, records
  `incident_requested`). Broadcast-only evidence is marked
  `evidence_trust: broadcast` and cannot be escalated without a note
  (T1).
- `GET /v1/violations` with the plan's filters, bbox through PostGIS on
  the excerpt's first position, paged.

## Tests

- Vectors: `alert_lifecycle.json` and `zones_vertical.json` through
  `RunOwned("authority")` over the track → `alerting.Track` mapping and
  the event → `violation/v1` mapping (the stateful sequences fed to one
  monitor per case; the adapter's output compared).
- Scenarios (`internal/ltest`, simulated receiver, real NATS and
  Timescale): SC-04 as a scenario (constant AMSL over a synthetic DEM
  that falls; `height_120m` raised when AMSL − DEM first exceeds 120 m,
  cleared after the hysteresis; the terrain source on the violation);
  SC-07 (registered and unidentified transmitters through a PROHIBITED
  zone: zone violation for both, `unregistered` for the unidentified,
  all three clear `resolved` together, every transition audited); SC-12
  (applicability windows judged at `captured_at`); SC-13 (PROHIBITED AGL
  zone, no DEM: warning with `limit_not_judged`, error-level status
  line; CONDITIONAL not evaluated and counted); SC-08 steps 2–5 (clear
  as `source_disabled` within milliseconds of the commit, re-raise
  within 1 s of re-enabling); `identification_mismatch` from a
  registered serial broadcast with another operator's number, on the
  ground too (G-02), and raising nothing from a `backlog` batch (T-04).
  Each scenario asserts the raise **and** the clear (INV-02, E-01) and
  the `events` rows.
- E-02: a worker with no zones and no terrain configured says so on
  every status line and judges nothing wrongly as clear.
- E-10: excerpt bound per violation; monitor capacity (`C-18`) exercised
  with a flood of spoofed ids: a real zone violation survives.
- Benchmarks `BenchmarkTrackMapping`, `BenchmarkEventMapping`.
