## What to build

- Realm `police`: accounts per agency with MFA, `agency`, `ip_allowlist`
  (CIDRs; a login from outside is refused and audited), scope
  `police.query`; no console roles; sessions as WP-2 with `realm` set;
  the picture WebSocket accepts the realm read-only (WP-13 checks
  `realm ∈ {console, police}`).
- Every query carries `purpose` (from a configured list, Q-A14) and
  `case_ref` (free text, required); both are stored in `police_queries`
  with the query, the result count and the user, and in `events`.
- `GET /v1/police/aircraft?bbox=&at=`: who is / was flying there, from
  the live picture (`at` absent) or `tracks` (`at` within retention),
  returning serial, registration public part, identification status,
  trust, times and positions; operator identity only when `purpose`
  allows PII per configuration, resolved through WP-3 with `pii_view`
  audit.
- `GET /v1/police/operators/{reg}` and `/serials/{serial}`: registry
  status, fleet, and identity under the same rule.
- `POST /v1/police/exports {incident_id | query}`: a `legal` evidence
  pack through WP-17's builder with purpose and case reference,
  hash-signed, download audited.
- Never any read of `occurrences.*` (the role has no grant; the package
  does not import it; a test proves both).
- DPO report `GET /v1/audit/dpo-report?month=` (admin, auditor): every
  PII view and export of the month with actor, purpose, case reference,
  entity, from `events` and `police_queries`.
- Rate limits per user and per agency (E-10).

## Tests

- E-01 pairs: inside and outside the allow-list; with and without
  purpose; a purpose that allows PII and one that does not; a realm

- Integration: a query produces exactly one `police_queries` row and

  occurrences.
