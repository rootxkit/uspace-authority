## What to build

- Client of `WS /v1/manned-traffic/stream?bbox=` over TLS with this
  system's client certificate (`MANNED_CLIENT_CERT`, `MANNED_CLIENT_KEY`
  from the environment) and an ecosystem token with scope `ansp.traffic`
  and audience `ansp`; bootstrap from `GET /v1/manned-traffic/snapshot?
  bbox=`; the bbox is configuration (Georgia or the designated areas).
  Reconnect forever with backoff (B-08).
- Each newline-delimited frame → `track/manned/v1` validated against
  the schema; `captured_at` from `PlaceBatch(rx_ts, ts[])` per frame
  (T-02), `time_source: provider`; `alt_pressure_m` and `alt_wgs84_m`
  kept apart (D-03; the picture shows which); `trust: surveillance`,
  `source: ansp_feed`, `source_instance: <adapter id from the frame>`.
  Published on `man.v1.<cell3>.<cell5>.<icao24>` and to the writer
  (`tsw.v1.manned_tracks`).
- Feed state: `healthy`, `stale` (no frame for 5 s), `unavailable since
  T` (socket down), `lagging` with `lag_s` (B-03, B-04: "unavailable" is
  not "lost"); on `src.v1.ansp_feed.<instance>` every 2 s and on the
  picture via WP-13. Source control through the WP-10 follower (type
  `ansp_feed`).
- Counters: frames, aircraft, refused (schema), clamped placements,
  reconnects.
