## What to build

- Client of `WS /v1/manned-traffic/stream?bbox=` over TLS; with
  `AUTHORITY_MTLS_MODE=required` it presents this system's client
  certificate (`MANNED_CLIENT_CERT`, `MANNED_CLIENT_KEY` from the
  environment), with `off` (staging, lab) it does not and says so at
  error level every status period (M25); plus an ecosystem token with
  scope `ansp.traffic` and `aud` = the ANSP's host (M18); bootstrap
  from `GET /v1/manned-traffic/snapshot?bbox=`; the bbox is
  configuration (Georgia or the designated areas). Reconnect forever
  with backoff (B-08).
- Every frame on the stream is the common envelope + `body` and is
  dispatched on `schema` (M12, M29): `track/manned/v1` bodies are
  validated against the ANSP's schema; `console/status/v1` is the
  feed's own status (the ANSP's `feed/status/v1` is retired) and its
  `degraded[]` / `sources[]` are copied onto this system's source
  status; state bodies the ANSP sends for an adapter (`stale`,
  `source_disabled`) age that adapter's tracks exactly as a local
  source switch would, never by removal; an unknown `schema` is counted
  and skipped (additive rule), never a disconnect. `captured_at` from
  `PlaceBatch(rx_ts, ts[])` per frame (T-02), `time_source: provider`;
  `alt_pressure_m` and `alt_wgs84_m` kept apart (D-03; the picture
  shows which); `trust: surveillance`, `source: ansp_feed`,
  `source_instance: <adapter id from the frame>`. Published on
  `man.v1.<cell3>.<cell5>.<icao24>` and to the writer
  (`tsw.v1.manned_tracks`).
- Feed state: `healthy`, `stale` (no frame for 5 s, including no
  `console/status/v1`), `unavailable since T` (socket down), `lagging`
  with `lag_s` (B-03, B-04: "unavailable" is not "lost"); on
  `src.v1.ansp_feed.<instance>` every 2 s and on the picture via WP-13.
  Source control through the WP-10 follower (type `ansp_feed`).
- Counters: frames, aircraft, refused (schema), clamped placements,
  reconnects.

  values; a frame without `rx_ts` placed at arrival and counted (T-12);
  `PlaceBatch` clamping counted; dispatch on `schema`: a
  `console/status/v1` frame, an adapter `stale` state, an unknown
  schema, each handled beside a `track/manned/v1` frame (E-01).

  `internal/ltest`: tracks on `man.v1`, rows written; the server
  closed for 30 s → `unavailable since T` frame on the picture and
  resumption without duplicates (E-02); disable by source control →
  disconnect and `source_disabled`.
